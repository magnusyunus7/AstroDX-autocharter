    How to align

    1. BPM: half/double of what you feel? Click another candidate or ×2 / ÷2. Wrong? Type it or tap T.
    2. Beat 1: pink bar lines on beat 2/3/4? Use ◀ beat / beat ▶ (, .), or pause on a downbeat and press O.
    3. Fine-tune: metronome on (M), play, nudge with [ ] (±1 ms, Shift ±10 ms) or Shift+drag the grid until clicks sit on the kicks. Slow the speed for precision.
    4. Check a later part of the song: drifting grid = BPM slightly off.
    5. Press Confirm timing.

    Wheel: scroll · Ctrl+wheel: zoom · Drag: pan · Click: seek · Space: play · ←/→: ±1 s (Shift ±0.1 s) · +/−: zoom
